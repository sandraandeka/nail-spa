    Have Questions?

    We're committed to making your experience as smooth and straightforward as possible.

    What Is A Manicure? +
    A manicure is a beauty treatment for the hands and fingernails. It includes nail shaping, cuticle care, hand massage, and polish application.
    What is the difference between Hard Gel and Soft Gel? +
    Hard gel is rigid, requires filing for removal, and is best for nail extensions. Soft gel is flexible, soak-off with acetone, and ideal for gel polish manicures.
    How Long Do Hard Gel Nails Last? +
    Hard gel nails typically last 2 to 4 weeks with proper care, and fills are recommended every 2-3 weeks to maintain strength and appearance.
    Can I get nail art with hard gel or soft gel? +
    Yes! Both hard gel and soft gel are perfect canvases for nail art, from French tips to custom designs.
